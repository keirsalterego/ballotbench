How to read this

  • You versus their usual is what matters. A 0.60 from a judge who gives most projects 0.50 is a strong review; the same 0.60 from a judge who gives most projects 0.70 is a weak one.
  • How much it counted depends on how consistently the judge's scores followed the other judges' on the projects they shared. A judge who gave every project the same score, or wrote only one review, tells us nothing about which project is better, so their review counts for nothing.
  • The middle is a pull towards an average score. It matters most for projects with few reviews, so that two generous reviews can't carry a project to the top on their own.
