Ranking

Pairwise is a second opinion: every judge who scored two projects differently is read as picking the better one, and the picks are ranked with a Bradley-Terry model. It ignores how far apart a judge put two projects, so no way of using the scale can move it. Where it and the calibrated rank agree, trust the rank more.

Calibrated scores are on the rubric's 0 to 1 scale. Could be is the range the rank lands in 90% of the time when each project's reviews are resampled and everything is refitted: a wide range means the scores can't tell this project from its neighbours.
